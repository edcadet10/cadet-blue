> Produce a governance, risk, and compliance package for a small organization, mapped to NIST CSF
> and the CIS Controls.

## Objective

Demonstrate the documentation and analysis side of security: identifying risk, mapping controls to
a framework, and writing the policies that operationalize them.

## Planned scope

- Scoped asset inventory + data classification
- **Risk register** (likelihood × impact, risk treatment)
- Control mapping to **NIST CSF** and **CIS Controls v8**
- **POA&M** (plan of action & milestones) for gaps
- Sample policies: Acceptable Use, Access Control, Incident Response

## Skills to demonstrate

NIST CSF · CIS Controls v8 · risk assessment & risk register · POA&M · security policy authoring · control mapping
